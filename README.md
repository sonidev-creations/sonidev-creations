## About

I'm an AI/ML student researcher pursuing my B.Tech in Information Technology at Nadar Saraswathi College of Engineering and Technology. My work centers on **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** and **Large Language Model (LLM)** systems, with a broader interest in Generative AI and applied machine learning research.

I'm currently interning at **ThisAI Technologies**, working on AI training data pipelines — cleaning, structuring, and annotating datasets used to train and evaluate AI systems.
